1.0.20: a report you chose to send now keeps trying if it can't reach the developer straight away, instead of being lost — retried in the background, with a SEND action in CONFIGURE > REPORTED OUTPUT for anything still undelivered. Reports you didn't opt to send are never transmitted. The report dialog's caption now names every field a send carries. Model downloads interrupted by the system resume where they left off rather than appearing stalled. Targets Android 16.
